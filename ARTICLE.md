# KAAPPAAN — காப்பான்: AI-Powered Maritime Safety

## Title
KAAPPAAN: Protecting Tamil Nadu Fishermen with AI-Powered Maritime Safety

## Description
AI-powered platform built with Strands Agents SDK and Amazon Bedrock Nova 2 Lite. Real-time GPS tracking, geofence monitoring, emergency SOS, and AI safety recommendations in Tamil for Bay of Bengal fishermen.

## Body

### The Problem
Tamil Nadu fishermen face maritime boundary violations, weather emergencies, and slow rescue coordination. Hundreds arrested annually, dozens of lives lost.
